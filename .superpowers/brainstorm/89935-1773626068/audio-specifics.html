Audio Quality — what matters most?

Pick everything you want improved (multi-select)

A

Aliasing at Extreme Rates

Fast forward / fast reverse sounds grainy or buzzy. High frequencies break up when the rate is pushed hard.

B

Rate Smoothing Tightness

The 5ms rate smoothing makes direction changes feel slightly behind. Could be faster/adaptive without adding clicks.

C

Clicks & Pops

Audible clicks when scratch starts, stops, or changes direction. The 1ms zero-crossing fade isn't always enough.

D

Lookahead Gap

The hardcoded 0.1s pre-roll means scratch always starts 100ms behind the live audio. Want it tighter or configurable.

E

Pitch Lock Mode

Scratching changes both speed and pitch (realistic). Option to decouple them — stretch time without shifting pitch (like CDJs in "slip" mode).

F

Interpolation Quality

Currently cubic Hermite (good). Upgrade to sinc/windowed-sinc for higher quality at slow rates, or add oversampling.
